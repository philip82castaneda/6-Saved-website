Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers

No sales script — just straight answers to the questions we hear most.

Will I get less than a Zillow estimate or listing price?

Typically Zillow reflects the property's condition if it were ready for a retail sale. This means it is reasonably updated without any major repairs needed. It also considers how fast a property can sell. If getting the highest possible price matters more to you than speed and certainty, listing with an agent may be the better move, and I will tell you just that.

Do I need to clean out the house first?

No. Leave whatever you don't want. We handle all of it.

What if I change my mind after I say yes?

You can back out before closing with no penalty, up until the date we agree on in writing. We'd rather you feel confident in your decision than feel pressured.

Are you my real estate agent?

No. We're a local buyer purchasing the property directly. We are not a licensed broker or agent representing you.

Who pays closing costs?

We do — all of them, every time. It's part of the offer.

How is my offer calculated?

We look at recent comparable sales in your area, the property's condition, and what it will realistically cost to bring it up to market-ready. Because of my 25 years in the trades, I know the expense necessary to bring the home up to retail ready. That is why the number I provide up front is the number we close at.

Do you buy houses that need major repairs?

Yes — that's a large part of what we do. Foundation issues, roof problems, fire or water damage, deferred maintenance, situations where the house is filled with personal items, or worse, refuse. — sell it exactly as it sits. We factor the condition into the offer rather than asking you to fix anything first.

What if I'm getting other offers too?

Do it. We'd rather you compare and know you are making the right choise. If another offer makes more sense for your situation, we will encourage you to take it.
