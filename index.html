Honest answers

Will I get less than a Zillow estimate or listing price?

Usually, yes — our offer reflects the property's condition, the speed, and the certainty of a cash sale, not full retail. If getting the highest possible price matters more to you than speed and certainty, listing with an agent may be the better move, and we'll tell you that honestly.

Do I need to clean out the house first?

No. Leave what you don't want. We handle it.

What if I change my mind after I say yes?

You can back out before closing with no penalty, up until the date we agree on in writing. We'd rather you feel sure than feel pressured.

Are you my real estate agent?

No. We're a local buyer purchasing the property directly for our own account, not a licensed broker or agent representing you.

Who pays closing costs?

We do — all of them, every time. It's part of the offer, not something you pay out of your proceeds.
