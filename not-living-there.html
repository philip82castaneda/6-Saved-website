The Old Way

What a traditional sale asks of you from a distance

Listing a house you don't live in the normal way usually means:

— Finding and vetting contractors for repairs, sight unseen, and hoping the work is done right
— Flying or driving in repeatedly for showings, inspections, and walkthroughs
— Paying property taxes, insurance, and utilities every month the house sits empty and unsold
— Coordinating with an agent, a buyer's agent, an inspector, and a lender you've never met, all by phone
— A buyer's financing falling through months into the process, sending you back to square one
— Clearing out decades of belongings on a deadline, often while grieving or managing a family transition at the same time
The Direct Way

What selling directly removes

✓ No repairs to coordinate — we buy the house as-is, in whatever condition it's in
✓ No showings to schedule or travel back for
✓ No need to clear out the house first — leave what you don't want, we handle it
✓ One point of contact throughout — me, Phil, not a chain of agents and inspectors
✓ A closing date that works for you and your family, not a buyer's mortgage timeline
✓ Our own crew (Cast Contracting) handles any work the house needs after closing — so our number doesn't move after a closer look
This is often more about time and peace of mind than the highest possible price. If getting full retail value matters more to your family than speed and simplicity, listing traditionally may be the better fit — and we'll say so honestly if you ask us.
